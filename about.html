My Story: Building Production AI Systems

My M.Tech in Data Science at IIT Jammu taught me to think rigorously about evidence. Early on, I built production NLP and multimodal AI for healthcare. What I learned: a model that's technically impressive but slow, expensive, or unreliable in production isn't actually done.

Since then, I've led PoC validation and built a RAG-powered enterprise assistant (10K+ daily users) and a regulatory intelligence platform (200+ sources, 18+ countries) — architecture, retrieval design, and the full data & AI pipeline behind both. That's the work I want to keep doing at a senior technical level: owning hard data & AI pipelines end to end, not handing the interesting parts to someone else.
